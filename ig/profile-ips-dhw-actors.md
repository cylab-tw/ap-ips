# IPS-DHW Actors

## Scope

This section defines actor responsibilities in wallet-based credential lifecycle operations.

## Actors

* IPS Issuer: issues signed IPS credentials
* Health Wallet: stores and presents credentials under patient control
* IPS Verifier: validates credentials and consumes IPS content for care

## Transactions

Actors participate in issue, present, verify, and optional identity binding flows.

## Options

Selective disclosure and offline presentation capabilities may be implemented per deployment strategy.

## Security

Actors shall protect key material, credential storage, and verification services with strong controls.

## Cross-Profile

Issuer implementations commonly align with IPSE creator capabilities and IPD identity expectations.
