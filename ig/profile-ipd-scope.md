# IPD Scope

## Scope

The IPD profile defines cross-border patient identity federation so that patient demographics can be synchronized and resolved consistently across participating systems and countries.

## Actors

* Patient Identity Source
* Patient Identity Manager
* Patient Identity Consumer

## Transactions

* Mobile Patient Identity Feed [ITI-93]
* Mobile Patient Identity Query [ITI-78]
* Subscribe to Patient Updates [ITI-94] (optional)

## Options

* Subscription Notification Option

## Security

All identity exchanges shall use secure transport and auditable access control because demographic records are personally identifiable information.

## Cross-Profile

IPD is the identity foundation for document discovery and retrieval in the IPSE profile.
